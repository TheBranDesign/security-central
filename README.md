# Security Central — Modern Enterprise Website Redesign

> **Personalized Wholesale Monitoring & Technology Demonstration prepared for Dan Turner, Chief Technology Officer, and Executive Leadership at Security Central.**

![Security Central Redesign](assets/logos/logo.svg)

## 1. Project Overview

**Security Central** (`https://www.security-central.com/`), established in 1963 by Ralph and Christine Brown and headquartered in Statesville, North Carolina, is one of North America's premier wholesale central-station alarm monitoring providers.

This redesign transitions Security Central's digital presence into a modern, high-tech, trustworthy enterprise platform that emphasizes:
- **60+ Years of Dedication**: The legacy, integrity, and 100% wholesale commitment that independent alarm dealers depend on.
- **Tri-Redundant Central Stations**: Live synchronized monitoring facilities across **Statesville, NC** (HQ Bunker), **Knoxville, TN**, and **Anderson, SC** to guarantee zero downtime.
- **Cutting-Edge Technology Suite (Spearheaded by CTO Dan Turner)**:
  - **ELA (Empathetic Logic Assistant)**: Next-gen intelligent conversational notification with automated voicemail detection and SMS dialogue.
  - **DENALI**: Real-time collaborative mobile portal empowering subscribers and keyholders to resolve alarms, chat in sync, and cancel false alarms with one touch.
  - **SCAN & SCAN NEO**: High-performance dealer management portal offering 5-second test mode toggles, live raw signal feeds, and accounting analytics.
- **Verified Credentials & Certifications**:
  - **TMA Five Diamond Monitoring Center** (100% certified operators)
  - **Underwriters Laboratories (UL Listed UL 827)**
  - **ASAP-to-PSAP** (Direct sub-second 911 CAD transmission)
  - **Factory Mutual (FM Approved)** commercial fire signaling
  - **IQ Certified** installation and service quality
- **Turnkey Dealer Support Services**:
  - White-label customer billing, ACH/card collections, and monthly payouts
  - White-label proactive customer outreach & surveys
  - 24/7/365 live dealer answering services & on-call technician dispatch
  - In-house hands-on Dealer Tech Lab troubleshooting all major panel brands

## 2. Interactive Features & Architecture

1. **Live Central Station Telemetry Simulator**: Real-time stream cycling verified CAD, UL Fire, and Denali events with latency metrics.
2. **Proprietary Technology Showcase**:
   - Interactive ELA conversational chat simulator.
   - Interactive Denali mobile response mockup with one-touch false alarm cancellation.
   - Interactive SCAN Neo dealer table preview.
3. **Filterable Solutions Portfolio**: Instant filtering across Wholesale Monitoring, Turnkey Billing, Customer Outreach, 24/7 Answering, and Tech Support.
4. **Interactive Risk Assessment & Dealer Onboarding Wizard**: 4-step interactive questionnaire evaluating dealer scale, service mix, and technology requirements, delivering immediate evaluation metrics.
5. **Incident Reporting Protocol Resources**: Authoritative life-safety guidelines distinguishing between 24/7 emergency voice hotlines (1-800-438-4171), SCAN test mode, and dealer escalation pathways.
6. **Mobile-First Responsive Design**: Optimized typography, smooth hamburger navigation drawer, and touch-friendly controls.
